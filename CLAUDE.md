# CLAUDE.md — The Creative Brain of Cotton Foundation

You are reading the brand brief for **Cotton Foundation**, an independent nonprofit that owns and operates **Taylor's Place**, a respite sanctuary for families facing terminal illness. This file exists so that any Claude session, Claude Design, or human designer can enter this repository with no prior context and produce work that is unmistakably Cotton Foundation.

Read this file completely before touching anything. Then read the files it points to. Do not rediscover the brand; it is documented here.

---

## 1. What Cotton Foundation is

Cotton Foundation is a 501(c)(3) (legal name, to be confirmed: The Cotton Charitable Foundation) whose mission is **restoring hope to children and families facing life-altering illness**. It does this through **Taylor's Place**: thirty acres west of Katy, Texas, with a main lodge (fishing, picnics, a pool, games, movies) and a second phase (a music hall, basketball and pickleball courts, a playground), where a family with a terminally ill member can rest, reconnect, and create lasting memories together. Taylor's Place is named for Taylor Odum, whose father, Tom Wallace, spent the last years of a four-year battle with cancer making memories with his family and giving to others. The Foundation funds Taylor's Place partly through **CrawFest**, an annual crawfish fundraiser.

**Cotton Foundation stands alone.** It has no affiliation with any other company or organization, and this repository contains no reference to any. Do not add one. Do not import copy, history, dates, marks, or colors from anywhere but this repository. The old website (cottonfoundation.org) was the source of the *visual* system only; its copy about anything other than the Foundation and Taylor's Place is not carried forward. See `brand/brand-architecture.md`, "Independence".

The one-line creative reference: **an established Texas foundation with a warm serif, a navy field, a gold light, and one place at the center of everything it does.**

## 2. The aspiration

Warmth without sentimentality; hope made practical. The bar is the best of American philanthropic identity: quiet, literate, permanent, generous with photographs and stingy with decoration. It should look like it could be cast in bronze on a gate and still feel kind on a phone screen.

The test for every piece of work:

> Would a family arriving at the Taylor's Place gate, a volunteer setting up CrawFest, and a donor reading the annual letter all recognize the same organization, and would each of them feel respected by how it looks?

If it looks like a generic charity template, a hospital brochure, a rustic ranch brand, a crawfish poster for anything but CrawFest, or a Canva logo, reject it.

## 3. Personality

Warm. Direct. Grounded. Established. Respectful. Hopeful. Practical. Independent.

In practice: real photographs, few colors, quiet type, room to breathe, one gold thing per screen, the family's dignity above the design. See `brand/brand-principles.md` and `brand/voice-and-tone.md`.

## 4. The identity that already exists (do not reinvent it)

### The mark (fixed)
An open cotton boll (Cotton Gold `#9F762B`) held from below by two open hands (Foundation Navy `#17345E`). Nearly square. It is the one image of the brand and it is **fixed**: not redrawn, not recolored within, not extracted into parts, not put in a shield. Files: `assets/marks/cf-mark_<colorway>.svg/.png` in full color, white, navy, gold, black.

### The horizontal lockup (fixed)
The mark at left; COTTON over a letterspaced FOUNDATION at right, in a Garamond-class serif (outlines; face unconfirmed, Q3). Ratio 3.118 : 1; measured proportions in `guidelines/logos.md` and `tokens/`. Files: `assets/logos/cotton-foundation/cf-lockup_horizontal_<colorway>.svg/.png` in full color, white, navy, black. **This is the primary logo.** It appears alone: never beside another organization's logo.

### The Taylor's Place mark (exists as built, not yet a file)
On the property's gate sign and a truck: a hand-drawn roofline silhouette over TAYLOR'S PLACE in serif capitals, CREATING MEMORIES in small sans capitals between rules, and the Cotton Foundation lockup beneath as the endorsement. Gold on navy. Reference photographs in `assets/logos/taylors-place/reference/`. The design phase digitizes it faithfully; it does not redesign it. See `guidelines/sub-brands.md` and `concepts/taylors-place/BRIEF.md`.

### The vectors
The SVGs are auto-traced from 6000–8334 px PNGs downloaded from the old site (the master files were not available). They are working vectors: production-ready at any practical size, regenerated by `tools/derive_from_originals.py`, never hand-edited, and replaced the day the owner supplies originals (Q1).

### What does not exist yet
A stacked lockup, a compact lockup, a descriptor template, an embroidery cut, PDF masters, the digitized Taylor's Place mark, a web component library, and every application. Full inventory and provenance: `assets/originals/INVENTORY.md`.

## 5. Brand architecture (the system, not a pile of logos)

```
COTTON FOUNDATION            the organization · the mark + wordmark · "Restoring Hope"
└─ TAYLOR'S PLACE            flagship property, owned and operated by the Foundation
│    roofline mark · "Creating Memories" · always endorsed COTTON FOUNDATION
└─ CRAWFEST                  annual event · benefits Taylor's Place · Foundation lockup always present
```

Any future program is a typographic descriptor under the lockup; any event is an edition. Nothing else gets a mark. Read `brand/brand-architecture.md` and `guidelines/sub-brands.md` before designing anything with a name on it.

## 6. Color

Foundation: **navy and gold on white.** Full specifications in `guidelines/colors.md`; machine-readable in `tokens/`.

| Role | Color | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Foundation Navy | `#17345E` |
| Primary (mark only) | Cotton Gold | `#9F762B` |
| Primary (eyebrows) | Harvest Gold | `#A07400` |
| Accent (calls to action, statistics on navy) | Hope Gold | `#E0A600` |
| Secondary | Horizon Blue, Signal Red | `#3E6B9C` `#BD202E` |
| Neutral | Ink, Slate, Silver, Mist, Cloud, White | `#17212E` `#808285` `#B1B1C2` `#F5F8FA` `#F3F3F3` `#FFFFFF` |

Rules: three golds with fixed roles, never swapped. Hope Gold always carries Ink text, never white. Body text is Ink, not Slate. Photographs under white type always sit beneath Foundation Navy at 72%. Never add a color.

## 7. Typography

Two faces, both open source on Google Fonts: **Cormorant Garamond 700** for headings, statistics, and anything that should feel warm and established; **PT Sans 400/700** for body, navigation, buttons, forms, and the brand's signature **eyebrow** (a short uppercase label in Harvest Gold above every heading). Heading line height 1.2 (the old site's 1.5 is too loose). Details in `guidelines/typography.md`. DM Sans is loaded on the old site and held as provisional (Q5); do not add a third face.

## 8. The visual language

- **Layout:** a 980 px content column, sections in the eyebrow + heading + copy rhythm alternating white, Mist, and navy; 24 px corners on cards, panels, and photographs; 8 px on buttons; one shadow. See `guidelines/layout.md` and `guidelines/components.md`.
- **Photography:** the land, the lodge, the families (with consent and dignity), the Foundation's people and events; natural light; the navy overlay under any type; no stock, no illness imagery. See `guidelines/imagery.md`.
- **Iconography:** one utility icon family; the heart only on donation buttons; at most two property glyphs (roofline, crawfish); no patterns. See `guidelines/iconography.md`.
- **Components:** header, hero, section opener, five button styles, cards, the navy impact band, split media, statistic list, sponsor row, CTA band, forms, footer, measured from the old site with seven fixes to apply. See `guidelines/components.md`.

## 9. References to use, and what to avoid

Use: the old site's *design* as measured in `references/website-audit.md` and shown in `references/website-screenshots/`; the gate sign; the Taylor's Place renderings and video for the place's character; the best of American foundation and land-trust identity for restraint.

Avoid: any copy, date, statistic, or name from the old site that is not about the Foundation or Taylor's Place; any other organization's identity; charity clichés (hearts, hands-and-globes, teal and orange); hospital and hospice aesthetics; rustic-ranch clichés for Taylor's Place; stock photography; black overlays and gradients; trendy fonts; Canva-style logos.

## 10. How to create something new and keep it Cotton Foundation

1. Start from the lockup (or the Taylor's Place mark with its endorsement). If neither is on it, ask why.
2. Open with an eyebrow, a Cormorant Garamond heading, and two sentences of PT Sans.
3. Navy field or white page; one Hope Gold action; a photograph under the overlay.
4. Remove one thing. Then remove another.
5. Check that no other organization's name or mark has crept in, and that no fact is unconfirmed.
6. Run the test in section 2. Then check `guidelines/brand-usage.md` for the don'ts.
7. Save explorations in `concepts/<name>/`, never in `assets/`. Approved work moves to `assets/` only after owner sign-off, recorded in `brand/decision-log.md`.

## 11. Things you must not invent

- A founding year or "Est." date. None is used. Do not write one.
- The mission statement's final wording and the legal name. Working versions are in `brand/brand-story.md`; confirm before printing (Q2).
- Statistics, dollar figures, contact details, opening dates. Placeholders only until confirmed (Q8, Q11).
- Taglines locked to marks. "Restoring Hope" and "Creating Memories" are working lines (Q4).
- Pantone, CMYK, and thread numbers marked unverified. Proof against physical samples.
- Any affiliation. There is none.

## 13. Repository rules

- Never edit, rename, or delete anything in `assets/originals/`.
- Vector masters (SVG, and PDF once produced) for every approved mark. Rasters are working files and previews.
- Kebab-case names. Asset files: `cf-<asset>[_<variant>]_<colorway>.<ext>`; `tp-` for Taylor's Place.
- Every directory has a README that says what belongs there.
- Record decisions in `brand/decision-log.md` with the date.
- Retire to `archive/`, never delete.
- Before every commit: `grep` the repository for any other organization's name; there should be none.
