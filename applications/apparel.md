# Apparel

Apparel identifies staff and volunteers, thanks donors, and gives families at Taylor's Place something to take home. It is not merchandise that advertises.

## Garment colors

Navy (primary), white, heather gray. Nothing else without a decision-log entry. Hope Gold is a print or thread color, never a garment color.

## Marks on apparel

| Garment | Front | Back or sleeve | Colorway |
|---|---|---|---|
| Staff polo | The mark alone, left chest, 60–65 mm wide, embroidered | Nothing, or the lockup across the yoke at 200 mm for event staff | White thread on navy; full color or navy thread on white |
| Volunteer T-shirt (events) | The mark alone, left chest, 75 mm, printed | The event graphic per `events.md`, or the lockup at 250 mm with "Benefiting Taylor's Place" | White on navy |
| Taylor's Place guest T-shirt and cap | The Taylor's Place mark with endorsement, centered chest at 200 mm (shirt) or the roofline + TAYLOR'S PLACE at 60 mm (cap front) | Nothing | Gold on navy (the sign's register) or navy on white; white on navy for caps |
| Quarter-zip, jacket | The mark alone, left chest, 60 mm, embroidered | The lockup on the back yoke, 180 mm, optional | White or tone-on-tone navy thread on navy |
| Cap (Foundation) | The mark alone, 55–60 mm wide, embroidered, front center | The site URL on the back strap, optional | White on navy; full color on white |

## Rules

- The mark alone is the default on chests and caps; the lockup goes on backs and yokes; the Taylor's Place mark goes on Taylor's Place pieces.
- Embroidery minimums (provisional until a sew-out): the mark 30 mm wide; the lockup 90 mm; the Taylor's Place mark 120 mm (the CREATING MEMORIES line is the constraint; drop it below that size).
- Thread: navy matched to 534 C, gold to 7557 C, from physical thread cards; low-sheen polyester; record the numbers in `guidelines/colors.md` after the sew-out.
- Print: one-color water-based or discharge inks on cotton; the mark's fine bracts need a 55+ mesh.
- No slogans on apparel except "Restoring Hope" (Foundation) and "Creating Memories" (Taylor's Place), each in Cormorant Garamond, and only after the taglines are approved (decision log Q4).
- No all-over prints, no sponsor logos on Foundation apparel except event volunteer shirts (sponsor row on the back, per `events.md`), no distressed or "vintage" treatments.

## Hats

Structured and unstructured caps in navy with the mark in white; a white or stone cap with the full-color mark for summer. Rope hats are acceptable for CrawFest. Leather patches (the mark debossed) are acceptable on caps for donors and staff.
