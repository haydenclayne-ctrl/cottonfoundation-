# Digital

## The new website

The website is the next phase after this design system (see `concepts/website/BRIEF.md`). It is built from `tokens/cotton-foundation.css` and the components in `guidelines/components.md`, and it replaces cottonfoundation.org.

**Pages (minimum):** Home · Taylor's Place · About · Support Us (donate, sponsor) · CrawFest · Contact · Blog or News · policy pages (Gift Acceptance, Refund, Terms of Use, Privacy).

**Rules:**
- The full-color horizontal lockup in the header on white; the white lockup alone in the footer. No other logo in either.
- The home hero belongs to Taylor's Place: its photograph, its gold call to action.
- The Taylor's Place page hero carries the Taylor's Place mark (once digitized) with the Foundation endorsement, then the property's story, phases, progress, and the sponsorship ask.
- One impact band per page, with placeholders until statistics are confirmed (Q8).
- Body text Ink, headings Navy, eyebrows Harvest Gold on light and Hope Gold on dark; heading line height 1.2.
- Fonts self-hosted (WOFF2) or from Google Fonts: Cormorant Garamond 700 (and 600 for print CSS), PT Sans 400/700.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA; the contrast table in `guidelines/colors.md`; visible focus rings in Navy; alt text on every photograph; captions on video.
- Performance: hero images under 300 KB, the mark as inline SVG, no carousels that autoplay.

## Donation flow

The donation platform is the owner's choice (Q11). Whatever it is, the hand-off from the site must not read as leaving the brand: the platform's page is themed with Foundation Navy, Hope Gold buttons with Ink text, the lockup at the top, PT Sans and Cormorant Garamond if the platform allows web fonts, and the tax-deductibility line with the confirmed legal name at the foot. Suggested gift levels are tied to Taylor's Place specifics (a family's stay, a night in the lodge, a piece of Phase 2) rather than abstract amounts.

## Email

- Single column, 600 px. Header: the full-color lockup on white, 180 px wide, linked to the site.
- One gold call to action per email (Ink text on Hope Gold).
- Sections follow the eyebrow + heading + copy trio at web sizes; photographs under the navy overlay when type sits on them.
- Footer: navy, the white lockup at 140 px, the confirmed contact details, unsubscribe, PT Sans 13 px Silver.
- Signature block (staff): name in PT Sans 700 Navy, title in PT Sans 400 Ink, the full-color mark at 40 px, phone and site in Navy links. No quotes, no banners.

## Favicon, avatars, app icons

- Favicon: the full-color mark on white, exported at 16, 32, 48, 180 (Apple touch), 192 and 512 px; the SVG mark as the modern favicon.
- Social avatars: the full-color mark centered on a white disc (or the white mark on a navy disc for dark-theme platforms), the mark at 70% of the disc.
- Taylor's Place accounts, if separate: the Taylor's Place mark (without endorsement) in gold on a navy disc.

## Templates

- Document (Word / Google Docs): letterhead per `print.md`, styles mapped to the type roles (Heading 1 = Cormorant Garamond 700 28 pt Navy, Body = PT Sans 11 pt Ink, Eyebrow = PT Sans 700 8 pt caps Harvest Gold).
- Presentation (16:9): per `guidelines/layout.md`.
- Spreadsheet: PT Sans, Navy header row with white text, Mist banding.

## Video

Title cards and lower thirds per `guidelines/imagery.md`: navy panels, Cormorant Garamond titles, PT Sans captions, the white mark at the corner. End card: navy, the white lockup, the site URL in PT Sans, a Hope Gold "Donate" line.
