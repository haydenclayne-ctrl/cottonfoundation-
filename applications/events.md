# Events

## CrawFest

The Foundation's annual crawfish fundraiser, benefiting Taylor's Place (continuation and venue pending decision log Q9). It is the one place the brand gets loud, and the rules below keep the loudness inside a frame.

**Fixed on every piece:**
- The Cotton Foundation lockup, white on navy or full color on white, unaltered, alone.
- The line "Benefiting Taylor's Place" (or "A Cotton Foundation event benefiting Taylor's Place" where there is room).
- Foundation Navy as the field for everything that is not the event graphic.
- PT Sans for all information (date, venue, tickets, sponsors); Cormorant Garamond for the event headline.

**Free within the event panel:**
- Crawfish photography, the red of a boil, a festive "CrawFest" lettering if the event already has one. If an event wordmark exists from past years, the design phase evaluates it; if not, "CrawFest" is set in Cormorant Garamond 700.
- The event's yearly theme graphic (a poster, a T-shirt back).

**Pieces:**

| Piece | Specification |
|---|---|
| Save-the-date and invitation | Navy, the event headline in Cormorant Garamond white, the date in Hope Gold, the lockup at the foot; the event graphic on the reverse |
| Poster and social announcement | Event graphic panel above, navy information panel below with the lockup and "Benefiting Taylor's Place" |
| Tickets and wristbands | Navy, white PT Sans, the mark; tier in Hope Gold |
| Sponsor wall / step-and-repeat | White field, sponsor logos at equal area in rows, the Foundation lockup and Taylor's Place mark on a separate navy band at the top. Sponsors are thanked, not co-branded |
| Stage backdrop and banners | Navy, the white lockup large, "Benefiting Taylor's Place", the event headline; no sponsor logos on the stage backdrop |
| Program / menu | Letter or A5, the trio rhythm, PT Sans lists |
| Event apparel | See `apparel.md`: the event graphic on the back, the mark small on the front |
| Thank-you post-event | A photograph from the night under the navy overlay, the total raised (confirmed) in Cormorant Garamond Hope Gold, the lockup |

## Other events

Sponsor dinners, ground-breakings, ribbon cuttings, and open days at Taylor's Place use the Taylor's Place mark with endorsement as the primary identity and the Foundation lockup on print and digital collateral. Same rules: navy field, one gold accent, photographs under the overlay, no other organization's logo except in a sponsor row.

## Sponsors at events

Sponsor recognition follows `guidelines/brand-usage.md`: their logos in their colors on white, equal area, grouped by tier with a PT Sans caps tier label; never inside the Foundation's navy panels, never locked up with the mark. Presenting sponsors get the first row and a larger area, not a place in the Foundation's lockup.
