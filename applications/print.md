## The Taylor's Place sponsorship catalog

The existing catalog is a landscape PDF hosted on the old site. The new edition follows the system:

- Cover: navy, the Taylor's Place mark in Cotton Gold with the Foundation endorsement, the edition date in PT Sans caps.
- Opener spreads: the story (Tom Wallace, Taylor Odum) with the archival photographs, eyebrow + heading + copy.
- Catalog spreads: one sponsorship item per spread or half spread; item name in Cormorant Garamond 700, amount in Cormorant Garamond 700 Hope Gold on a navy panel, description in PT Sans, a rendering or photograph with 24 px (6 mm) corners, a "what your gift builds" line.
- Back cover: the lockup, the tax line with the confirmed legal name, contact details.
- 16:9 landscape, exported for screen and for print (with bleed).

## Donor and appeal materials

- Annual letter: letterhead, one photograph, one impact band as a navy panel with white statistics (confirmed figures only), the gold "Give" call to action as a URL and QR code.
- Appeal brochure (tri-fold): cover panel navy with the white lockup and a Taylor's Place photograph; inside the trio rhythm; reply panel with the tax line.
- Certificates and plaques for named gifts: Cormorant Garamond for the donor name, PT Sans for the description, the Taylor's Place mark with endorsement, gold on navy or navy on cream.

## Event print

Tickets, table cards, programs, and sponsor signs for CrawFest and other events follow `events.md`: the Foundation lockup on every piece, "Benefiting Taylor's Place" on every piece, event graphics contained to their own panel.

## Production notes

- Vector files from `assets/` for every mark; never place a PNG in a print file when the SVG exists.
- Minimum lockup width in print 40 mm; use the mark alone below that.
- Proof Pantone and CMYK against swatches; record confirmed values in `guidelines/colors.md` (decision log entry).
- Foil: Cotton Gold matte or satin foil (a 7557 C match), never mirror gold.
