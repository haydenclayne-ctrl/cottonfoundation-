# Signage

Taylor's Place is a physical place, and its signage is where the brand is most permanent. The gate sign already exists and sets the register; everything else follows it.

## The gate (existing)

A dark panel (navy/black) mounted on the black pipe gate carrying the Taylor's Place mark in gold: the roofline, TAYLOR'S PLACE, CREATING MEMORIES between rules, and the Cotton Foundation lockup as the endorsement. Photographs in `assets/logos/taylors-place/reference/`. Match this sign's color and material in the field before specifying anything new; confirm whether it is cut metal, routed, or printed (owner, decision log Q1).

## The entrance arch

The old site's rendering shows TAYLOR'S PLACE lettered across an arch between stone pillars. If built, the lettering is the mark's serif capitals in cut metal, letter height sized to the arch (minimum 25 mm per 10 m of viewing distance from the road), with the roofline glyph optional at the arch's center. No dates, no additional words.

## Wayfinding and buildings

| Sign | Specification |
|---|---|
| Directional (drive, parking, lodge, pond, courts) | Navy panels, white PT Sans 700 caps at +100 tracking, an arrow in the same weight; the mark small at the top of the post |
| Building identification (The Lodge, The Music Hall) | Navy panel or cut letters on the building; name in the serif capitals; the Taylor's Place mark on the primary entrance only |
| Room and door plates | Brushed metal or navy acrylic, PT Sans, 25 mm letters; the mark not repeated on every door |
| Donor recognition wall | Navy field, names in Cormorant Garamond, tiers in PT Sans caps, the Taylor's Place mark with endorsement at the top in gold; see `print.md` for plaques |
| Memorial plaque (Tom Wallace) | Bronze or gold on navy, Cormorant Garamond, the roofline glyph; text approved by the owner and the family |
| Regulatory and safety | Standard code signage; brand type only where code allows |

Materials: painted or powder-coated steel in navy, brushed brass or gold-finished aluminum for lettering, Cor-Ten or stone for bases; navy acrylic and vinyl for interior. No printed banners as permanent signs.

## Vehicles

- Dark vehicles: the Taylor's Place mark with endorsement in white on the tailgate (as built) or the door, 300–400 mm wide; the Foundation lockup in white on the front doors of Foundation vehicles.
- White vehicles: the full-color lockup on the doors; the Taylor's Place mark in navy on the tailgate.
- Matte or satin films. No wraps, no photography on vehicles, no phone numbers larger than the mark.

## Event and temporary signage

Banners, feather flags, and A-frames for CrawFest and events: navy field, white lockup, one line of Cormorant Garamond, event graphics contained to a separate panel (`events.md`). Sponsor walls per `guidelines/brand-usage.md`.

## Production notes

- Vector files only; the gate sign's own artwork is to be supplied or digitized before any matching piece is cut (Q1).
- Letter heights: 25 mm per 10 m viewing distance minimum; the roofline glyph never below 150 mm wide on signage.
- Color: Cotton Gold and Foundation Navy matched to physical samples (paint, vinyl, powder coat) recorded in `guidelines/colors.md`.
