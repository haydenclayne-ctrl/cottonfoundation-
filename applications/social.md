# Social

## Profiles

- Avatar: the full-color mark centered on a white disc (mark at 70% of the disc); the white mark on a navy disc where the platform's dark theme makes white discs glare.
- Name: Cotton Foundation. Handle: @cottonfoundation (existing on Instagram).
- Bio: one sentence in the voice ("Restoring hope for children and families facing life-altering illness, through Taylor's Place.") and the site link. No emoji strings, no hashtags in the bio.
- Cover / header images: a Taylor's Place photograph under the navy overlay with the white lockup at the left third, or the plain white lockup on navy.
- If Taylor's Place has its own accounts: the Taylor's Place mark in gold on a navy disc as the avatar, bio ending "Owned and operated by Cotton Foundation."

## Post templates

| Template | Construction |
|---|---|
| Photo post (1:1, 4:5) | The photograph full frame; the white mark at the lower right corner at 8% of the width, inside the clear space; no text on the image unless it is the caption template |
| Caption-on-image | The photograph under the navy overlay on the lower third only (a gradient is not permitted; use a solid navy band at 85% opacity), one line in Cormorant Garamond 700 white, the eyebrow above it in Hope Gold caps, the white mark at the corner |
| Statistic post | Navy field, the figure in Cormorant Garamond 700 Hope Gold at 40% of the height, the label in PT Sans 700 white caps, the white lockup at the foot; the mark watermark at 10% |
| Quote post | Mist field, the quote in Cormorant Garamond 700 Navy, the attribution in PT Sans Ink, the full-color mark at the foot |
| Event post | Per `events.md`: event graphic panel above, navy information panel below |
| Story (9:16) | The photo template with the navy band at the bottom and the eyebrow in Hope Gold; stickers and platform type kept off the mark's clear space |
| Thank-you (sponsors, donors) | White field, "Thank you" in Cormorant Garamond Navy, the sponsor logos at equal area, the full-color lockup at the foot |

## Rules

- Photographs per `guidelines/imagery.md`: real, consented, no illness imagery, no stock.
- Type per `guidelines/typography.md`: two faces, no platform display fonts on brand posts.
- Colors: navy, white, Hope Gold, Mist. The crawfish red only inside CrawFest event graphics.
- The mark is never cropped, tilted, or placed on a busy area; the clear-space rule applies at every size.
- Captions in the voice (`brand/voice-and-tone.md`): warm, direct, grounded; one hashtag set (#TaylorsPlace #CottonFoundation #RestoringHope, once the taglines are approved) rather than a wall of tags.
- No content that references any other organization's name, work, or history.

## Video and reels

Vertical video keeps the top and bottom sixths clear for the platform's UI; captions burned in PT Sans 700 white on a navy band; the end card is the white lockup on navy with "Benefiting Taylor's Place" or the site URL.
