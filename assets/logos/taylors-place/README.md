# assets/logos/taylors-place/

The Taylor's Place mark. **It exists as built** (the gate sign at the property west of Katy, the decal on the Foundation's truck) but no digital artwork was available from cottonfoundation.org on 2026-09-22 and the owner has not yet supplied the sign artwork (decision log Q1). Until it is digitized in the design phase (`concepts/taylors-place/BRIEF.md`), this folder holds only references.

## The mark, as observed

1. **Roofline** — one filled silhouette of the lodge roof: a long low pitch rising left to right, a chimney at the right, a stepped gable. It sits above the wordmark, slightly wider than it.
2. **TAYLOR'S PLACE** — Garamond-class serif capitals, letterspaced, typographic apostrophe.
3. **CREATING MEMORIES** — small sans capitals, tracked wide, between two short horizontal rules, centered.
4. **COTTON FOUNDATION** — the Foundation's horizontal lockup, centered beneath at about 40% of the wordmark width. The endorsement is part of the mark on every standalone use.

Colorways as built: Cotton Gold on navy/black (sign); white on black (decal).
