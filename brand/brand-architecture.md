# Brand Architecture

The single most important idea in this repository: **Cotton Foundation is one independent organization with one mark, and Taylor's Place is the flagship of what that organization does.** Taylor's Place has its own name and its own sign, but it belongs to, is funded by, and is operated by the Cotton Foundation, and every piece of design says so.

## Independence

Cotton Foundation and Taylor's Place stand on their own. The Foundation's identity does not reference, borrow from, or co-brand with any other company or organization. Its history, its mission language, and its visual system are its own. Copy from the old website that tied the Foundation to any other organization's history or partners, or to lines of work other than Taylor's Place, is not carried forward (decision log D2, D11). In practice:

- The Foundation lockup appears alone. There is no co-brand lockup, no "a company of," no second logo in the footer.
- No other organization's colors, type, or marks enter the system.
- No "since" dates or anniversary claims derived from anyone else's history.
- Sponsors and partners appear only as logo rows on sponsorship and event materials, never as co-brands.

## The tiers

| Tier | Name | Role | Identity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Cotton Foundation** | The organization. Legal name The Cotton Charitable Foundation, a 501(c)(3). Public name Cotton Foundation | The mark (cotton boll in hands) and the horizontal lockup; navy, gold, white; Cormorant Garamond and PT Sans | Approved, in use |
| 1 | **Taylor's Place** | The flagship property and program: a private respite sanctuary on thirty acres west of Katy, Texas, for families navigating terminal illness. **Owned and operated by Cotton Foundation** | Its own mark (roofline + TAYLOR'S PLACE + CREATING MEMORIES) with the Foundation lockup as an endorsement beneath it. Same palette and type as the Foundation | Mark exists as built (gate sign, truck); to be digitized |
| 2 | **CrawFest** | The annual crawfish fundraiser whose proceeds benefit Taylor's Place | Yearly event graphics; the Foundation lockup always present; "Benefiting Taylor's Place" | Event materials exist outside this repository; continuation to be confirmed (Q9) |
| 2 | **Future campaigns** | Sponsorship drives, capital campaigns for Phase 2, memorial gifts | Titles in the Display Serif on Foundation-branded pages and posts. No marks | Rules only |

New programs, if the owner creates any, slot into tier 1 as typographic descriptors under the Foundation lockup. New events and campaigns slot into tier 2. Only the owner can create a new property with its own mark, and it would be built the way Taylor's Place is: same palette, same type, one glyph, the Foundation endorsement beneath.

## How the tiers relate

```
COTTON FOUNDATION
│   the mark + wordmark · "Restoring Hope"
│
├── TAYLOR'S PLACE ─────────── flagship property (owned and operated by the Foundation)
│      roofline mark · "Creating Memories" · endorsed "COTTON FOUNDATION"
│      ├─ Phase 1: the main lodge (fishing, picnics, the pool, games, movies)
│      └─ Phase 2: music hall, basketball and pickleball courts, playground
│
└── CRAWFEST ───────────────── annual event · benefits Taylor's Place
       event graphics · Foundation lockup + "Benefiting Taylor's Place"
```

- The **Foundation** identity is used when the subject is the organization: the website header and footer, letterhead, the annual letter, the donation portal, staff apparel.
- The **Taylor's Place** identity is used when the subject is the place: the gate, the lodge, wayfinding, the property's vehicles, guest materials, the sponsorship catalog, the Taylor's Place page hero.
- **CrawFest** graphics are used for the event only and change yearly.

## Naming conventions

- **Cotton Foundation** in lockups and headers; *Cotton Foundation* or *the Cotton Foundation* in sentences. The legal name only in tax and legal lines.
- **Taylor's Place** with the typographic apostrophe, always two words, always capitalized; TAYLOR'S PLACE only inside its mark.
- **CrawFest** one word with a capital F (as the old site wrote it; some event materials used "Crawfest" — the design phase fixes one spelling, decision log Q10).
- Never CF, TP, or "the Foundation" as a standalone brand name outside of copy that has already said Cotton Foundation.
- Campaign titles are written in Title Case in the Display Serif and are never abbreviated.

## What the architecture is protecting against

- Taylor's Place drifting into a separate charity in donors' minds (the endorsement rule prevents it).
- Any other organization's identity attaching itself to the Foundation (the independence rule prevents it).
- A logo for every campaign and event (descriptors and edition rules prevent it).
- Three golds becoming five (the color roles prevent it).
- Renderings, stock, and crawfish red taking over the visual voice (the imagery rules prevent it).
