# Brand Story

Everything in this file is drawn from cottonfoundation.org (the Foundation's previous website) as it read on 2026-09-22, limited to what belongs to Cotton Foundation and Taylor's Place. The old site also carried history, statistics, and program copy that are **not** part of this brand going forward (decision log D11); none of it is repeated here. Where a fact is uncertain it is logged for the owner (`decision-log.md`). Nothing has been embellished.

## In one sentence

Cotton Foundation restores hope to children and families facing life-altering illness, through Taylor's Place.

## Mission

The old site's mission statement covered work the Foundation no longer describes itself by. The working statement, pending the owner's final wording (decision log Q2), is the old statement with that clause removed:

> Cotton Foundation is dedicated to restoring hope for children and families facing life-altering illnesses. Our mission goes beyond rebuilding structures — we focus on rebuilding lives, spirits and communities.

The footer's shorter form, similarly adjusted: *Cotton Foundation's mission is to Restore Hope to children and families affected by illness.*

## The flagship: Taylor's Place

**Taylor's Place** is a private sanctuary on thirty acres west of Katy, Texas, where families with a terminally ill member can find respite and create lasting memories together. Phase 1 is the main lodge: fishing, picnics, the pool, games, movies, a house filled with comfort. Phase 2 adds a music hall, basketball and pickleball courts, and a playground. Ground was broken in 2025; the old site anticipated welcoming the first family in winter 2025 and opening officially in early 2026. **Cotton Foundation owns and operates Taylor's Place.**

The site's own words: *"A cornerstone of our mission is Taylor's Place, a private sanctuary designed to give families navigating terminal illness the space to rest, reconnect and create lasting memories together."* And: *"Taylor's Place is the heart of the Cotton Foundation — a safe haven where families can heal, rebuild, and thrive."*

## The namesake

Taylor's Place was inspired by **Tom Wallace**, a beloved friend and colleague. During a four-year battle with cancer he gave his time to his friends, his family, and to others, knowing that his wife and four-year-old daughter, Taylor, would cherish the memories he made with them in his last years. The Foundation built this haven in his memory: *"Tom Wallace was a man of great determination, a positive spirit, and a caring heart — a beloved friend, brother, father, and husband. The unforgettable memories and lifelong bonds created at Taylor's Place will be his fitting legacy."*

**Taylor Odum**, his daughter and the namesake, studied biology and chemistry at New York University and is pursuing a career in oncology.

## The event

**CrawFest** is the Foundation's annual crawfish fundraiser: music, fellowship, and thousands of pounds of crawfish, with proceeds benefiting Taylor's Place. CrawFest 2025 raised $760,000 (as the old site stated it). Whether CrawFest continues in its current form, and where, is for the owner to confirm (decision log Q9).

## The phrase

"Restoring Hope" is the Foundation's idea: the verb in its mission statement and the headline of its old home page. "Creating Memories" is Taylor's Place's line, cut into its gate sign. Neither is confirmed as an official tagline (decision log Q4), but both are in the ground and in the copy, and the design system treats them as the working taglines of their tiers.

## What the story asks of the design

Warmth without sentimentality. The subject is hard (terminal illness, a father who died young) and the brand's job is to make hope feel practical: a lodge with a pool, a pond to fish, a music hall, a plate of crawfish that pays for a family's week. The serif carries the warmth; the sans carries the practicality; navy holds it steady; gold is the light in it.
