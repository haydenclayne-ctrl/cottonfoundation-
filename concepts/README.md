## How to work here

- One subfolder per subject. Inside, date-stamped exploration folders: `explorations-YYYY-MM-DD/`.
- Every exploration folder gets a short `NOTES.md`: what was tried, what the recommendation is, what is unresolved.
- File naming as in `assets/README.md`, with `_exploration` or a round number as the variant: `tp-mark_endorsed_r2_gold.svg`.
- Always show new lockups **beside the existing horizontal lockup at the same mark width**, and the Taylor's Place mark beside its gate-sign photograph. Faithfulness to what exists is the test.
- Present in the brand colorways on white and on navy. Never on a gradient, a mockup with lens flare, or a stock lifestyle photograph.
- Rounds: r1 wide (3–5 directions where a choice exists), r2 narrowed (1–2), r3 refined (1). Owner approval after r3 only.
- Nothing here references any other organization; the independence rule in `brand/brand-architecture.md` applies to explorations too.
