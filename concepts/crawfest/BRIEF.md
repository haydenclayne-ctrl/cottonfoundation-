# Brief: CrawFest

**Subject:** the Foundation's annual crawfish fundraiser benefiting Taylor's Place (continuation and venue pending decision log Q9). The event gets a framework, not a logo: a fixed frame that carries the Foundation, and a free panel that carries the party.

## Deliverables

1. **The frame.** A template family (poster 24 × 36 in, social 1:1 and 9:16, banner 3:1, ticket, table card, program) with the fixed elements locked: the Foundation lockup (white on navy or full color on white), "Benefiting Taylor's Place", the navy information panel in PT Sans, and the event headline position in Cormorant Garamond 700.
2. **The event panel.** The area inside the frame where the yearly graphic lives. Define its proportions per template and its rules: crawfish photography or a boil-red field are allowed; the Foundation's marks are never recolored or placed inside it.
3. **"CrawFest" lettering.** Evaluate whether an event wordmark from past materials exists and should be kept. If not, set CrawFest in Cormorant Garamond 700 and stop; do not draw a new event logo.
4. **Sponsor recognition.** The sponsor wall / step-and-repeat and the program sponsor page per `applications/events.md`: white field, equal-area logos in tier rows, the Foundation and Taylor's Place marks on a separate navy band. Presenting-sponsor treatment: first row, larger area, never in the lockup.
5. **Apparel.** The volunteer shirt (mark on the chest, event graphic on the back, sponsor row beneath it) and a cap.
6. **The post-event thank-you** post and email: photograph under the overlay, the confirmed total in Cormorant Garamond Hope Gold, the lockup.

## Acceptance

- Cover the event panel with your hand: what remains is unmistakably a Cotton Foundation piece.
- "Benefiting Taylor's Place" appears on every template.
- Any earlier event badge or logo is retired; only the Foundation lockup represents the host (decision D5).
- No other organization's name or mark appears except in the sponsor rows, at equal area.

## Output

`concepts/crawfest/explorations-YYYY-MM-DD/` with `NOTES.md`; template files; one board showing the full family with a sample year's graphic in the panel.
