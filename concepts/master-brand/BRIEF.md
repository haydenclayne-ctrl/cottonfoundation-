# Brief: Master Brand

**Subject:** complete the Cotton Foundation mark and lockup family from the two formats that exist. The mark and the horizontal lockup are fixed (`guidelines/logos.md`); everything below is built from their parts without altering them.

## Deliverables

1. **Vector masters.** If the owner supplies original artwork (Q1), convert it to the export set and retire the traced SVGs. If not, review the traced SVGs in `assets/` at 100%, 400%, and 1600%, and fix only genuine tracing artifacts (a kink, a missed hole) by re-tracing with adjusted parameters, not by hand-editing paths. Produce PDF masters for every colorway.
2. **Stacked lockup.** The mark centered above COTTON / FOUNDATION (the wordmark artwork unchanged, scaled so that COTTON equals the mark's width or the mark equals FOUNDATION's width; test both). Gap between the mark's bottom and COTTON's cap top in the range 120–180 units (mark = 1000). For hats, avatars, and square placements.
3. **Compact lockup.** The mark beside a single-line COTTON FOUNDATION for widths under 200 px: either COTTON FOUNDATION set from the wordmark's own letters at FOUNDATION's tracking, or the mark with COTTON alone. For the mobile header, email signatures, and small print.
4. **Descriptor template.** The construction in `guidelines/sub-brands.md` for any future program name under the lockup, drawn once with a placeholder name, in PT Sans 700 caps.
5. **Small-format test.** The mark at 16, 24, 32, and 48 px, and at 8 mm printed. Decide whether a simplified cut (thicker bracts, fewer notches) is needed below 24 px, and if so draw it as a separate `_small` variant, never replacing the mark.
6. **Embroidery and cut simplification.** One `_embroidery` cut of the mark and of the horizontal lockup for thread, vinyl, and engraving: bracts and fingers evened to a minimum 1 mm stroke at the minimum size, holes closed where they would not sew.
7. **Colorway sheet.** Every approved format in every approved colorway on white and on navy, on one board, with the not-approved combinations shown crossed out.
8. **Clear-space and minimum-size diagrams** for the guidelines, drawn to the measured numbers in `tokens/`.

## Acceptance

- The mark's geometry is byte-for-byte the traced (or supplied) master in every deliverable; only scale, position, and color change.
- The stacked and compact lockups read as the same family as the horizontal lockup at the same mark width.
- Nothing is added: no rule under the wordmark, no tagline, no date, no frame.
- Files named per `assets/README.md`; explorations in `concepts/master-brand/explorations-YYYY-MM-DD/` with `NOTES.md`.
