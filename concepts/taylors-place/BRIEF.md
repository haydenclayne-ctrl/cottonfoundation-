# Brief: Taylor's Place

**Subject:** the flagship property, owned and operated by Cotton Foundation. Its mark exists as built (the gate sign, the truck decal) and nowhere as a file. This brief digitizes it faithfully, completes its formats and colorways, and specifies its property applications. It does **not** redesign the mark.

## Read first

`guidelines/sub-brands.md` (the mark as observed, the rules, the endorsement construction), `assets/logos/taylors-place/README.md`, and the two reference photographs in `assets/logos/taylors-place/reference/`. Then the gate-sign screenshot in `references/website-screenshots/taylors-place-03-construction-and-crawfest.jpg`.

## Deliverables

1. **Digitized mark.** From the gate-sign photograph (perspective-corrected) and, if supplied, the sign shop's cut file (decision log Q1): the roofline silhouette as a single vector shape; TAYLOR'S PLACE set in the confirmed wordmark serif (Q3; Cormorant Garamond if it matches) at the sign's letterspacing; CREATING MEMORIES in PT Sans 700 caps at the sign's tracking, between two rules; the Foundation horizontal lockup as the endorsement at the measured proportion (about 40% of the wordmark width, gap 120 units). Present the vector overlaid on the photograph to prove the match.
2. **Formats.** `tp-mark_endorsed` (the full mark with the endorsement, the default for standalone use); `tp-mark` (without the endorsement, for use where the Foundation lockup is already present on the page); `tp-mark_compact` (roofline + TAYLOR'S PLACE only, for caps, small print, and favicons of any Taylor's Place accounts).
3. **Colorways.** Cotton Gold on navy (the sign), white on navy, navy on white, black utility. No full-color version is needed; the mark is one color by construction.
4. **Export set.** SVG, PDF, PNG 2000 px, previews on white and navy, per `assets/README.md`. Names: `tp-mark_endorsed_gold.svg`, `tp-mark_white.svg`, `tp-mark_compact_navy.svg`, and so on.
5. **Property applications.** From `applications/signage.md`: the entrance arch lettering (if built), directional signs, building identification for the lodge and the music hall, the donor recognition wall, the Tom Wallace memorial plaque, the vehicle tailgate and door layouts. Drawings with dimensions, materials, and colors matched to the existing sign.
6. **Guest and sponsor pieces.** The guest T-shirt and cap (`applications/apparel.md`); the sponsorship catalog cover and a catalog spread template (`applications/print.md`); a certificate for named gifts.
7. **The Taylor's Place web page hero.** The mark with endorsement over a photograph of the land under the navy overlay, the eyebrow CREATING MEMORIES, the H1, the sponsorship call to action, per `guidelines/components.md`.

## Acceptance

- Laid over the gate-sign photograph, the digitized mark matches the sign in every proportion; differences are only the photograph's perspective.
- The endorsement is present on every standalone use; the Foundation lockup inside it is the approved artwork, unaltered.
- Same navy, same gold, same faces as the Foundation. No new colors, no script, no added imagery (no tree, sun, heart, or star).
- Nothing on any Taylor's Place piece references another organization.

## Output

`concepts/taylors-place/explorations-YYYY-MM-DD/` with `NOTES.md`; vector files; a presentation board per deliverable including the photograph overlay and in-situ mockups of the gate, a building sign, and the tailgate.
