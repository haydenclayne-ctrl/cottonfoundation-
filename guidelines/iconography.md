# Iconography

The brand has one illustrated symbol, the cotton boll in two hands, and one property glyph, the Taylor's Place roofline. Everything else drawn is a utility icon and should look like one.

## The mark is not an icon set

Do not extract pieces of the mark (the boll alone, one hand, the bracts) to make icons, bullets, or decorations. The mark is used whole or not at all. The one permitted decorative use is the whole white mark as a watermark at 8–12% opacity on navy panels (`logos.md`).

## Utility icons

The old site uses Font Awesome 5 (solid) for its handful of UI icons: the heart in "Donate Now", the phone, the clock, the map pin, the search magnifier, the play button, the carousel arrows, and the social glyphs (LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, Instagram). Rules:

- One family. Keep Font Awesome (or switch the whole set to another single-weight library in the design phase); never mix libraries.
- Solid style, sized to the text they sit beside (16 px next to 16 px type), same color as that text.
- The heart is the donation glyph. It appears only on donation buttons; it is not used decoratively and not used as a "like".
- Social icons: white glyph in a 40 px circle with a 1 px Harvest Gold ring on navy (footer); Navy glyph on white elsewhere.
- No icons in headings. No icon-plus-label grids as a substitute for photography.

## Property glyphs (design phase)

If the new site needs a small glyph for navigation or cards, the design phase may draw exactly two in a single 1.5 px line style at 24 px: a roofline for Taylor's Place (derived from its mark) and a crawfish for CrawFest. Anything beyond these needs a decision-log entry. They are navigational aids, never marks.

## Patterns

None. The cotton boll does not tile; the brand does not use a repeat pattern, a texture, or a border. Photography and the navy panel are the only "textures".

## Charts and statistics

Statistics are typographic (the impact band, the statistic list), not pictographic. If a chart is ever needed (an annual report, a capital-campaign progress bar), use Foundation Navy for the primary series, Hope Gold for the highlighted series, Silver for the rest, PT Sans for all labels, and no 3D, gradients, or icons in the chart.
