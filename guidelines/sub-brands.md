# Sub-Brands: Taylor's Place and CrawFest

Cotton Foundation is the organization. Everything it does carries the Foundation's mark, palette, and type. One thing it does has a name and a mark of its own: Taylor's Place. This document is the rulebook for how Taylor's Place (and the annual event that funds it) relate to the master brand, so that Taylor's Place is unmistakably *of* the Cotton Foundation and nothing ever reads as a separate charity.

## The hierarchy

```
COTTON FOUNDATION                          the organization (legal: The Cotton Charitable Foundation)
│  mark + wordmark; navy, gold, white; Cormorant Garamond + PT Sans
│  stands alone: no co-brands, no other organization's mark beside it
│
├─ TAYLOR'S PLACE                          flagship property and program
│     owned and operated by Cotton Foundation
│     has its own mark (roofline + wordmark + "CREATING MEMORIES"), always endorsed by the Foundation
│
└─ CRAWFEST                                annual fundraising event (edition), benefits Taylor's Place
      event graphics change yearly; the Foundation lockup is always present
```

See `brand/brand-architecture.md` for the independence rule and the tiers.

## Tier rules

| Tier | Gets its own mark? | Gets its own colors or type? | Endorsement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cotton Foundation** | Yes: the mark and lockup | The system | — |
| **Taylor's Place** (flagship property) | Yes: the existing roofline mark | No. Same palette, same faces. Its register leans warmer: more gold-on-navy, more photography of the land | "COTTON FOUNDATION" lockup beneath the mark, always, on every standalone use |
| **CrawFest** (event) | An event graphic, redesigned yearly | Event artwork may use the crawfish red and photography; the Foundation lockup stays in brand colors | "Benefiting Taylor's Place · A Cotton Foundation event" |
| Any future program or campaign | No, unless the owner decides it is a property | No | Written as a descriptor: the Foundation lockup with the name set beneath it |

The test: a donor who sees a Taylor's Place hat, a Foundation letter, and a CrawFest poster in the same week should recognize one organization behind all three.

## Taylor's Place: the existing mark

Taylor's Place has a mark that exists **as built** on the property's gate sign and on a Foundation truck (photographs in `assets/logos/taylors-place/reference/`). No digital artwork was available from the old website (decision log Q1). The mark, as observed:

1. **The roofline.** A single, hand-drawn silhouette of the main lodge's roof: a long low pitch rising left to right to a peak, a chimney at the right, a smaller gable stepping down. It sits above the wordmark like a hat, slightly wider than the word beneath. Drawn as one filled shape, as if cut from the sign.
2. **TAYLOR'S PLACE.** A serif in capitals, letterspaced, in the same Garamond class as the Foundation wordmark (the sign's letterforms have the same slanted serifs and oval O). The apostrophe is typographic.
3. **CREATING MEMORIES.** A small sans-serif line in capitals, tracked wide, set between two short horizontal rules, centered under the wordmark. This is the property's tagline.
4. **The endorsement.** The Cotton Foundation horizontal lockup, centered beneath, at roughly 40% of the wordmark's width.
5. **Colorway as built:** gold on a navy/black field (the gate sign); white on black (the truck decal).

This is a complete, coherent mark and it is already in the ground. The design phase **digitizes it** (draws it as vector from the reference photographs and, if supplied, the sign shop's cut file), locks its proportions, and produces the colorways. It does not redesign it. See `concepts/taylors-place/BRIEF.md`.

### Taylor's Place: the rules

- The mark is always shown with the Cotton Foundation endorsement lockup unless the Foundation lockup is already the page's header (a Taylor's Place page on the Foundation's website, for example, where the header carries the Foundation).
- Colorways: Cotton Gold on Foundation Navy (hero, signage, formal print); white on navy; Foundation Navy on white. Full color is not needed; the mark is one color by construction.
- Writing: *Taylor's Place* in sentences, with the apostrophe; TAYLOR'S PLACE in the mark. Never "Taylors Place", never "TP".
- Its tagline "Creating Memories" belongs to Taylor's Place only. The Foundation's phrase is "Restoring Hope".
- It never gets a different navy, a different gold, or a script face. The roofline is its only illustrative element; do not add a tree, a sun, a heart, or a star.

## Descriptors: any future program

If the owner adds a program or campaign, it has no mark. It is written as part of the Foundation:

- **Stacked descriptor:** the Foundation horizontal lockup, a gap of 25% of the mark's width, then the NAME in PT Sans 700 capitals, tracked 0.10 em, cap height equal to FOUNDATION's cap height, left-aligned with COTTON.
- **Inline descriptor:** on banners, the name in the Display Serif beneath or beside the lockup at heading size.

The design phase draws the descriptor template once (`concepts/master-brand/BRIEF.md`) so that any future name is set identically.

## CrawFest: the edition rule

CrawFest is an annual event. Its graphics are free to be festive (crawfish photography, the red of a boil, a script "CrawFest" if one is already established by the event's materials) but three things are fixed: the Cotton Foundation lockup appears in white or full color, unaltered; the line "Benefiting Taylor's Place" appears; and the palette outside the event graphic is the Foundation's. Any earlier event badge or logo from past years is retired; new CrawFest materials use the Foundation lockup (decision D5). Whether the event continues, and where, is for the owner to confirm (decision log Q9).

## Endorsement construction (for the Taylor's Place mark or any future property)

Units: sub-brand wordmark width = 1000.

| Element | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Gap, sub-brand mark bottom to endorsement top | 120 |
| Endorsement (Foundation horizontal lockup) width | 400 (range 350–450), centered |
| Endorsement minimum width | 200 px on screen, 40 mm in print (the lockup's minimum); below that, use the Foundation mark alone at 25% of the wordmark width |
| Color | Same single color as the sub-brand mark |

These numbers are read from the gate sign and should be confirmed against the original sign artwork when it is supplied.

## Naming in lockups and copy

- Cotton Foundation, Taylor's Place, CrawFest (one word, capital F).
- "The Cotton Foundation" with the article is acceptable in sentences ("Taylor's Place is the heart of the Cotton Foundation"); the lockup never carries "The".
- Never "CF" or "TP" in anything public.
- The legal name, The Cotton Charitable Foundation, appears only in the tax-deductibility line and legal documents.
