# Claude Design Brief: Cotton Foundation Creative Phase

**To:** Claude Design (or any designer taking the creative phase)
**From:** the Cotton Foundation brand repository, prepared 2026-09-22
**Subject:** turn the existing Cotton Foundation identity, as pulled from the old website and the built Taylor's Place signage, into a complete, disciplined design system, ready for the new website

## Read first, in this order

1. `CLAUDE.md` (the whole brand in one file)
2. `brand/brand-architecture.md` and `guidelines/sub-brands.md` (Cotton Foundation owns and operates Taylor's Place; the Foundation stands alone)
3. `guidelines/logos.md`, `colors.md`, `typography.md`, `components.md`
4. `assets/originals/INVENTORY.md`, then open `assets/marks/cf-mark_full-color.svg`, `assets/logos/cotton-foundation/cf-lockup_horizontal_full-color.svg`, and the two photographs in `assets/logos/taylors-place/reference/`
5. `references/website-audit.md` and the screenshots (design only; the copy is superseded)
6. `brand/decision-log.md` (the open questions; do not resolve them silently)
7. The `concepts/*/BRIEF.md` for each piece of work
8. `applications/` as you need them

## Non-negotiables

- **The mark and the horizontal lockup are fixed.** Use the files in `assets/`. Do not redraw the boll, the hands, or the wordmark. New formats are built from these parts.
- **Foundation Navy `#17345E` and Cotton Gold `#9F762B` are the brand.** The full palette in `guidelines/colors.md` is complete; do not add colors. Three golds have fixed roles.
- **Cormorant Garamond and PT Sans.** Nothing else.
- **Cotton Foundation stands alone.** No other organization's name, history, mark, or colors appear anywhere: no co-brand lockups, no "since" dates, no footer partner logos. Sponsors are a logo row on event and sponsorship materials only.
- **Taylor's Place is the flagship and is the Foundation's.** Its existing mark is digitized faithfully, never redesigned, and always endorsed by the Foundation lockup on standalone use.
- **One system.** Any future program is a typographic descriptor; events are editions. No new logos.
- **Do not invent facts.** No founding year, no statistics, no contact details, no taglines locked to marks, no Pantone or thread numbers as confirmed. Use placeholders and ask.
- **Nothing you make is approved until the owner says so.** Save everything in `concepts/<subject>/explorations-YYYY-MM-DD/` with `NOTES.md`. Never write into `assets/originals/`.

## Order of work

1. `concepts/master-brand/BRIEF.md`: vector masters (or review of the traced ones), the stacked and compact lockups, the descriptor template, the small-format test, the embroidery cut, the colorway sheet. **Everything else depends on this.**
2. `concepts/taylors-place/BRIEF.md`: digitize the Taylor's Place mark from the gate sign, complete its formats and colorways, specify the property applications, design the Taylor's Place page hero.
3. The web component library from `guidelines/components.md` and `tokens/`: header (with the compact lockup), hero, section opener, buttons, cards, impact band, split media, statistic list, sponsor row, CTA band, forms, footer, with the seven fixes at the end of `components.md` applied. This is what the website phase will build from.
4. `concepts/crawfest/BRIEF.md`: the event framework.
5. Applications from `applications/`: digital templates (email, social, avatars) first, then print (stationery, the sponsorship catalog), then signage and apparel.

## The acceptance test for every board

> Would a family arriving at the Taylor's Place gate, a volunteer setting up CrawFest, and a donor reading the annual letter all recognize the same organization, and would each of them feel respected by how it looks?

And the reject list: a generic charity template, a hospital brochure, a rustic ranch brand, a crawfish poster for anything but CrawFest, three golds fighting, black type on an unfiltered photo, a footer with two logos, a Canva logo.

## How to present

- Every new lockup beside the horizontal lockup at equal mark width; the Taylor's Place mark overlaid on the gate-sign photograph.
- On white and on navy. Real photographs of the land in mockups, under the navy overlay; no stock, no lens flare.
- Three rounds: wide, narrowed, refined. Recommend at each round; do not present ten options without a point of view.
- Deliver SVG and PDF for every mark; PNG previews; presentation boards as PDF; `NOTES.md` with what was tried, what you recommend, what is unresolved.
- Name files per `assets/README.md`.

## What to ask the owner before you get too far

Q1 the original vector files and the sign artwork · Q2 mission wording, legal name, and dates · Q3 the wordmark typeface · Q4 the taglines · Q5 one sans or two · Q6 the contrast fixes · Q7 three golds or two · Q8 photography and statistics · Q9 CrawFest · Q11 contact details and donation platform · Q12 the independence reading. All in `brand/decision-log.md`.
